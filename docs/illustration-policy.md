# Illustration Policy

## Rules for this series

1. **Default: no animate beings.** Prefer scenes, places, objects, nature, architecture, calligraphy and geometric ornament.
2. **People, when the story needs them:** simple figures (stick figures or flat silhouettes) with **no facial features at all**: no eyes, nose or mouth. Ideally the head is a plain shape or the figure is seen from behind or far away.
3. **Animals:** avoid. If a fable needs one, show its trace instead (footprints, a shadow, a nest, an empty cage) or a featureless silhouette.
4. **Prophets and sahaba: never drawn in any form**, not even as a faceless figure. Show the setting only: the place, the boat, the well, the desert, the city.
5. **No angels or the unseen** in any visual form.
6. One fixed style sheet (palette, line weight, texture) for the whole series.

## Basis

Scholars differ on images of living beings. The summary below is research to inform each reader's own decision, not a fatwa; ask a teacher you trust.

* **Hanafi position:** the head is the deciding factor. An image is excused when the head is removed, the face erased, or the figure is too small for features to be seen. Removing only eyes or limbs is not enough in this reading. [SeekersGuidance](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/what-makes-an-image-complete-in-the-hanafi-school/)
* **Children's educational books:** some scholars permit faceless characters for children's education, citing the precedent of Aisha's dolls. [SeekersGuidance](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/may-i-print-a-childrens-book-whose-characters-have-incomplete-features/), [IslamQA 87720](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/87720)
* **Caution:** IslamWeb advises blurred or absent faces to stay clear of the disagreement. [IslamWeb](https://www.islamweb.net/en/printfatwa.php?id=380557)
* **Prophets:** depicting prophets' bodies even with hidden faces is disallowed by most contemporary scholars. [IslamQA 315944](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/315944)

Rules 2 and 4 follow the cautious side of each point: no facial features at all, and nothing for prophets.
